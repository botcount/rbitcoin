Changed

- **The RPC, REST and Esplora limits added since 0.9.0 now have flags.**
  The RPC/REST response write floor is `--rpc-min-drain-rate BYTES`
  (conf `rpc_min_drain_rate=`, default 16384): a write may stall for
  30 seconds, and its total stalled time must stay under that plus
  bytes-written/rate, so a slow tunnel reading `getblock` at verbosity
  2 or 3, or `getrawmempool true`, is no longer cut at a fixed rate.
  The built REST reply budget is `--rest-reply-bytes BYTES` (conf
  `rest_reply_bytes=`, default 67108864): raise it so a reply larger
  than 64 MiB, such as a full `/rest/mempool/contents.json`, is not
  refused while another reply drains. An Esplora request body must
  arrive within `--esplora-body-read-timeout SECS` (conf
  `esplora_body_read_timeout=`, default 30, 1 to 86400). Accepted
  Esplora sockets are `--esplora-accept-multiplier N` (conf
  `esplora_accept_multiplier=`, default 8) times
  `--esplora-max-inflight`; a socket past that cap is closed with no
  HTTP reply. NixOS: `services.rbitcoin.esplora.acceptMultiplier`,
  `services.rbitcoin.esplora.bodyReadTimeout`,
  `services.rbitcoin.rpc.minDrainRate` and
  `services.rbitcoin.rpc.restReplyBytes` (null omits the flag).
  Outspends replies now take the node's single heavy turn above
  `--heavy-sh-creates` entries, as unpaged scripthash joins do,
  instead of above a fixed 10,000 entries whatever that flag said;
  `0` turns the turn off for outspends too. A page of outspends is
  still at most 10,000 entries. (#2089)
